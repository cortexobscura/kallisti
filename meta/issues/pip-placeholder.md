# Picture-in-picture: no picture in the page meanwhile

## Summary

While a stream plays in picture-in-picture, its tile in the page keeps
playing too. Other players (YouTube) show a note there instead.

## Requirements

- The tile shows "Playing in picture-in-picture" and a button to bring it
  back while that stream floats.

## Acceptance Criteria

- In picture-in-picture the tile shows the note, not the picture; back
  from it, the picture shows again.
