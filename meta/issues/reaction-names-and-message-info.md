# Who reacted, and message info (also on phones)

## Summary

Report (2026-10-26): in a mobile browser there is no way to see who
reacted to a message (desktop shows it on hover only). Wish: an info
entry per message, as in Signal, with the date and the edit date.

As in Discord, a long press on a reaction opens a sheet with a tab per
emoji and the names. As in Signal, "Info" in a message's actions shows
when it was sent, edited and pinned, and its reactions with names.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A long press on a reaction opens the reactions sheet at that emoji; a
  tap still toggles one's own reaction.
- "Info" in the long-press sheet on phones and in the message actions on
  desktop: sent, edited (if), pinned (if), reactions with names.
- Times in the user's 12- or 24-hour setting.
