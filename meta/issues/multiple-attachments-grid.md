# Image grid and a viewer with next and previous

Parent: [Several files in one message](multiple-attachments.md)

## Summary

Several images (and videos) of a message show as a grid as in Discord:
2 side by side, 3 as one big and two small, 4 as 2x2, more in rows of
3. Other files show as file cards below. The viewer goes through the
message's images (arrow keys, buttons, swipe on touch screens).

## Acceptance Criteria

- Grid layouts for 1 to 10 images, also on a phone.
- The grid keeps its size while the images load (no jump in the chat).
- The viewer shows "n of m" and goes to the next and previous image.
